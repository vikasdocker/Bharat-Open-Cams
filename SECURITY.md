# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please do not publish exploitable details, credentials, access tokens, or private camera URLs in public issues. Use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting feature if enabled for this repository; otherwise contact the repository maintainer privately through an established channel.

## Scope

Report security issues involving this repository, its catalog validation, clients or infrastructure.

## Camera feed safety

Do not probe, scan, log into, or test arbitrary third-party cameras as part of a report. Provide the official source page and describe the issue without exposing private video or personal data. If a camera feed appears unintentionally exposed, notify its owner/operator or appropriate authority rather than copying or redistributing it.

## Maintainer response

Treat credible claims of unauthorized publication as a reason to suspend the catalog entry while reviewing evidence. Never publish secrets or private authorization correspondence.
