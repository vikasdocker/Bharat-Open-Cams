# Public Traffic Webcam Access and Usage Request

Use this as a draft for an official public enquiry. It has **not** been sent. Confirm the recipient and current contact address on the authority's official website before sending.

## Suggested first enquiry: Mumbai Traffic Police

- Official CCTV page: https://trafficpolicemumbai.maharashtra.gov.in/cctv/
- Official CCTV/traffic-management initiative: https://trafficpolicemumbai.maharashtra.gov.in/en/initiative/
- Official contacts page: https://trafficpolicemumbai.maharashtra.gov.in/en/contact/
- The contacts page lists the Joint Commissioner of Police (Traffic), Mumbai; verify the current contact details there before sending.

## Draft email

**Subject:** Request for officially public traffic-webcam feeds and permitted integration details — Bharat Open Cams

Respected Sir/Madam,

I am developing Bharat Open Cams, a free public directory intended to help residents view traffic and public-interest camera views that the relevant authority has intentionally made available to the public.

We reviewed the Mumbai Traffic Police CCTV pages, but could not identify a public-facing live camera feed or terms authorizing third-party integration. We do not want to access internal control-room feeds or cameras that are only exposed on a network.

Could your department please advise whether any official traffic webcams, public snapshot endpoints, or public viewing pages are available for citizen use? If so, we would appreciate confirmation of:

1. The canonical public viewing URL or documented API.
2. Whether the source may be listed in an independent, free Android app and website.
3. Whether linking to the official page is permitted, and separately whether embedding, proxying, or redistribution is permitted.
4. Required attribution, refresh rate, caching rules, rate limits, and usage restrictions.
5. The process for written permission, source corrections, and removal requests.
6. Whether any feed includes personal or enforcement-only content that must not be publicly republished.

We will not scan camera networks, attempt credentials, bypass access controls, record footage, or publish private/residential cameras. We will list a source only after its official publication status, permissions, and permitted viewing method have been documented and reviewed.

Thank you for your time and guidance.

Regards,  
Bharat Open Cams project maintainer  
Repository: https://github.com/vikasdocker/Bharat-Open-Cams
