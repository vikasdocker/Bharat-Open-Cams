# Terms of Use

**Applies to:** the current Bharat Open Cams project prototype.

## Nature of the service

Bharat Open Cams is an independent directory project intended to help people find public camera views intentionally published by official source owners. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by any Indian government authority.

## Third-party sources

Each camera remains under the control of its publisher. The publisher's own terms, notices and technical restrictions govern access. A listing is not a guarantee of continuous availability or a claim that the feed is owned by this project.

The project must not list a source unless its publisher, permission evidence, permitted viewing method and attribution have been reviewed. Public reachability alone is not authorization to redistribute a stream. When permission is unclear, the source must not be published.

## Acceptable use

Do not use this directory to identify, track, target, harass or surveil private individuals. Do not try to bypass authentication, geo restrictions, embedding restrictions, rate limits or other source-owner controls. Do not copy or record feeds unless the publisher's terms explicitly allow it.

## Reporting an issue

Use the repository issue template to suggest an official public source. For a concern about an existing listing, provide its catalog ID and the official publisher page, without posting private video, credentials or personal data. Entries may be suspended or removed while a permission concern is reviewed.

## Availability and changes

Sources can become unavailable or change their terms without notice. The project may correct, suspend or remove directory entries. The current prototype is provided as-is and does not promise nationwide coverage, uninterrupted playback, or a particular number of available feeds.

These terms are a project draft, not legal advice. They require qualified review before a production launch.
